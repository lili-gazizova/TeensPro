# TeensPro — Your Future, Your Path

**Building AI final project**

## Summary

TeensPro is a proposed AI-powered career and education guide for teenagers aged 14–17 in Belarus, Georgia, Armenia, Kyrgyzstan, and Tajikistan. It helps users explore careers, find study options, and learn about scholarships and other funding opportunities.

## How it works

1. Users share their interests, education level, and language skills.
2. TeensPro suggests career areas and explains why they may be relevant.
3. It filters study and funding opportunities using each programme’s requirements.
4. It explains requirements in simple language and links to official sources.
5. Users can create a checklist of next steps.

## Data and AI methods

The planned data sources are official university, education, and scholarship websites. Examples include [Erasmus Mundus](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters), [Chevening](https://www.chevening.org/resource-hub/guidance/eligibility/), and [DAAD Scholarships](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/daad-scholarships/).

TeensPro plans to use:
- **Rule-based filters** to check basic eligibility.
- **Recommendations** based on users’ interests and goals.
- **NLP** to help find details such as deadlines and required documents.
- **An LLM** to explain verified information in simple language.

Important details must be checked against official sources. Missing information should be marked as unknown, not guessed.

## Challenges and safety

Programme rules and deadlines can change, so information needs regular checks. The platform should collect as little personal data as possible and protect users’ privacy, especially because many users are minors.

TeensPro gives guidance, not guarantees. It cannot guarantee admission, a scholarship, a visa, or a job. Users should confirm details with official sources and discuss important decisions with a trusted adult or education adviser.

## Next steps

- Build a small catalogue of careers and verified opportunities.
- Test the idea with teenagers, parents, and education advisers.
- Check information accuracy and the usefulness of recommendations.
- Add language support and more opportunities when reliable information is available.
